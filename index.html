What was broken

A template site that looked thrown together in an afternoon, with half the sections dead when you tapped them. For a firm whose whole job is getting an injured person to make contact in the first few minutes, a link that goes nowhere is a lost case.

What we built

A single-purpose intake site. The case-review form captures accident and injury details and lands straight in their inbox. Above it, a three-step guide to the first days after a crash, built around Florida's 14-day PIP deadline and the two-year filing limit, plus an FAQ that answers the cost and insurance questions people ask before they call. Phone, text and form all reachable in one tap, and the whole site runs in English and Spanish.
