How filming works

By default, we handle all of it. We film and produce the content ourselves, so you don't need a camera, a crew, or a free afternoon — nothing lands on your plate.

If you'd like one of your own staff in a video, we can make that work too. We send whoever's going on camera a short, step-by-step guide, they record the clips on their phone in a few minutes, and we take it from there. Either way, it costs nothing extra.

If you'd rather we come to you and film on site, we can. On-site visits are made on request and billed at our hourly rate plus travel.
